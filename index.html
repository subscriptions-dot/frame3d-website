Frame3D Spatial Browser.

A browser for Meta Quest that turns web pages, photos and videos into 3D while you browse. The depth runs on the headset.

Get it for Quest

Every flat page
gets a second eye.

You see depth because your two eyes look from two places. A web page only gives you one view. Frame3D builds the second one for the photos and videos on the page, so they gain real depth inside your headset.

What it does
Spaces01/ 02 Lounge

A calm sunset lounge with a round sofa and soft light. Your windows float in it while you browse.

Real-time in the headset

What it does.

One browser for the whole web in your headset: pages and photos in depth, VR video, rooms to browse in, web games and the add-ons you already use.

Photos • Pages • Video

2D → 3D, live

Frame3D makes a depth map for what you're looking at and gives it a second eye.
VR video

Plays the way it was filmed

180° and 360° video wrap around you. Side-by-side 3D video shows each eye its own half.
Spaces

Lounge and Aurora

Play with a pad
