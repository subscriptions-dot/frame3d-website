Everything in the browser

Features.

Frame3D is a full web browser for Meta Quest. Here is what it does, grouped so you can find it fast.

3D & videoBrowsingMake it yoursPlay & controlPrivacyReviews

3D and video

The reason Frame3D exists: flat pictures and video get real depth, worked out on the headset.

2D → 3D

Live depth on any page

Photos and videos on a page get a depth map and a second eye, about 38 depth maps a second on the headset's own GPU.

One tap

A 3D button on every photo and video

Each picture and video the page shows gets its own 3D button, so you choose what turns 3D.

VR video

180°, 360° and side-by-side

VR video plays the way it was filmed: 180° and 360° wrap around you, side-by-side shows each eye its half.

Media

Your videos and photos

One page for the videos and photos on the headset and what you played recently.

Browsing

A real browser first: tabs, windows and the add-ons you know.

Windows

Multi-window

Pull a tab out of the tab strip into its own window and keep several pages around you.

Add-ons

Firefox add-ons

Install real add-ons from addons.mozilla.org right in the headset.

Power users

Userscripts

Add your own scripts to change how sites look and work.

Ad blocker

Built-in blocker

Blocks adverts with standard filter lists you can subscribe to.

Make it yours

Rooms and looks that feel like your own space.

Spaces

Lounge and Aurora

Browse in a calm sunset lounge or under the northern lights instead of a grey void.

Themes

Firefox themes and colours

Use the built-in looks, install Firefox themes, or pick your own colour on a colour wheel.

Play and control

Use what is in your hands.

Games

Gamepad and controllers

Pair a Bluetooth gamepad or use the Quest controllers. Inside a game, every other button is the game's.

Keyboard

Bluetooth keyboard shortcuts

Pair a keyboard and use the shortcuts every desktop browser has.

Voice

Hinglish voice

Search, type or give commands by speaking, in English or in Hinglish.

Privacy

What you do stays on your headset.

Private

Private browsing

Open private tabs, or clear browsing data when you exit.

Diagnostics

You decide what to share

A diagnostic summary is made only when you ask for it, and it leaves out web addresses, history and cookies.
