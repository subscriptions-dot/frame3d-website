Privacy

Frame3D is a web browser for Meta Quest. It has no Frame3D account, no cloud sync and no analytics. What you do in it stays on your headset.

What stays on your headset

  • Browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, saved Spaces and settings.
  • The list of your downloads. The files themselves are saved by the system download manager.
  • Add-ons and user scripts you choose to install, and their data.
  • Depth maps. The 2D-to-3D depth is worked out on the headset's own GPU; pictures and video frames are not sent anywhere for it.

What leaves your headset, and why

  • The websites you open. Like any browser, Frame3D connects to the sites you visit. They may use cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies.
  • Add-ons. When you install an add-on or theme from addons.mozilla.org, it is downloaded from Mozilla's servers.
  • Depth models. The first time a depth model is needed, it is downloaded from GitHub or Hugging Face.
  • Updates. The version installed from GitHub checks GitHub for a newer release. Store versions are updated by Meta instead.

Frame3D does not sell, share or upload your browsing data.

Camera, microphone and location

When a website asks for the camera, microphone or your location, Frame3D denies it unless you allow it. Voice commands are used only when you start them.

Diagnostics

A diagnostic summary is made only when you press Export diagnostic summary in Settings. It is saved as a file on your headset and holds the app version and general app state. It leaves out web addresses, page titles, history and cookies. You decide whether to share it.

Your controls

  • Clear history and the download list in Settings.
  • Use private browsing, or clear browsing data when you exit.
  • Uninstalling Frame3D, or clearing its storage, removes everything it stored.

Children

Frame3D is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: open an issue on the Frame3D releases page. Because Frame3D keeps no account and no server copy of your data, there is nothing stored with us to delete.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It is a static page.

To show it, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when they send the files, under their own policies. The download button takes you to GitHub.
